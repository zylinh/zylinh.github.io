Research
Interests

I am interested in intelligence that understands both the physical world and the human mind: models that can simulate how the world responds to actions, and read how people think and act. My current focus:

  • Interactive Video World Models

    Video generation models that serve as world simulators, responding faithfully to actions and staying consistent with what they have shown over long horizons.

  • 3D Animation Generation

    Generating controllable and editable animation for diverse rigged 3D assets, with agents that understand skeletal structure and plan motion.

  • Vision-Language Navigation

    Building embodied agents that ground human demands into concrete destinations and executable flight plans for UAVs in city-scale environments.

  • Social Interaction Understanding

    Enabling vision-language models to understand nonverbal cues such as gaze, infer people’s intentions and mental states, and anticipate their next actions in social interactions.

Previously, I worked on generative AI for renewable energy, building synthetic datasets for solar panel segmentation.
