About Research Publications

Zhengyuan Lin 林政源

Master’s student · UT-Vision, The University of Tokyo

I am a master’s student in Information and Communication Engineering at the University of Tokyo, working in UT-Vision under the supervision of Prof. Yusuke Sugano. I am also an AI research intern at Alaya Lab, working on interactive video world models.

Previously, I was a research assistant at the Hong Kong Polytechnic University, working with Prof. Zhiling Guo in DigiEnergy Lab. I received my bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Technology from South China Normal University.

Research
Interests

I am interested in intelligence that understands both the physical world and the human mind.

My research so far has explored Social Interaction Understanding, Interactive Video World Models, and Embodied AI.
