Research
Interests

I am interested in intelligence that understands both the physical world and the human mind. My research so far has explored:

  • Social Interaction Understanding. How vision-language models interpret nonverbal cues such as gaze to infer human intent and mental states. I study attention misalignment in VLMs’ gaze understanding and am collecting a multi-view, multi-person social interaction dataset, since complementary viewpoints reveal cues a single view misses.
  • Interactive Video World Models and 3D Animation. Data engines, benchmarks, and models for worlds that respond to actions, including synthetic exploration data with rich annotations (WorldRover) and action and memory evaluation (WorldMark), as well as agentic text-guided animation of diverse rigged 3D assets.
  • Embodied AI. City-scale UAV vision-language navigation that grounds abstract human demands into destinations and executable flight plans.
